YOUR FEELINGS BELONG HERE.
NOTICE THE LITTLE CHANGES

You don’t need
perfect words
to be heard.

Some days it’s a few words. Some days it’s “I’m tired.” Sometimes it’s noticing that today feels a little easier. Each is a part of your story.

Adltrack’s check-in concept makes space for symptoms, energy, and the things you want to remember for your next conversation with your care team.

YOUR STORY, IN YOUR WORDS

A little pause.
A little perspective.

There’s value in making a moment for yourself. In noticing what changed. In remembering that you’re more than a checklist.

THOUGHTFULLY, ONE STEP AT A TIME
01

Start where you are.

Make room for how today actually feels, without needing to make it sound better.

02

Keep what matters.

Capture the observations and questions you want to remember.

03

Bring yourself into the conversation.

Use your own words to talk about your experience with the people supporting your care.
