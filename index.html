What is Adltrack?+

Adltrack is a concept for a friendlier care companion: medication reminders, appointment organization, symptom check-ins, and a circle of people you choose. Read about everyday care and support above.

Who is it for?+

Anyone keeping up with care after an appointment, managing an ongoing routine, or supporting someone they love.

Can my loved ones see everything?+

The concept puts sharing in your hands. This website introduces the concept; it does not share personal information with other people.

Can I use it for real medical reminders?+

This website is a product preview. It does not send notifications, book appointments, or provide medical advice.
