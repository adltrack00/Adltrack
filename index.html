Good to know.

Does Adltrack give medical advice?

No. Adltrack only shows what the discharge papers and the pharmacy labels say. For anything else, call the doctor or the pharmacist. In an emergency, call 911.

Can I get reminders when Adltrack is closed?

Yes. Allow notifications and daily reminders arrive even when Adltrack is closed (on iPhone, add it to your Home Screen first). You can also export them to your calendar.

Who can see the care plan?

Only the people an admin adds: family and paid caregivers. Each person gets their own sign-in, and the admin chooses what they can do.

What if the papers and the bottle don’t match?

Call the pharmacy before giving it. Adltrack flags the difference and never picks one for you.

I don’t have the discharge papers.

Start with the bottles. Scan each pharmacy label, and add the papers later if you get them.
