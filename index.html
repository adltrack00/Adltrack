What is Adltrack?+

A care app for the weeks after a hospital stay. Scan the discharge papers, check the medications, and Adltrack turns them into reminders, refills, visits and a daily report the whole family can see.

Who is it for?+

Families caring for someone at home, the paid caregivers who help them, and anyone else you add, near or far.

Can everyone see everything?+

Everyone you add sees the same daily report, photos and alerts. You choose, person by person, who can give doses, add tasks or change medications. Everyone else just views.

How much does it cost?+

Solo is $9 a month for one person on their own. Family is $19 a month for up to 5 people, and Care team is $29 a month for 6 to 10.

Does it replace my doctor?+

No. Adltrack keeps track of what the hospital and your doctor told you to do. It doesn’t give medical advice, so always follow your doctor and pharmacist, and call 911 in an emergency.
