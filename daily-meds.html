ONE LIST FOR THE WHOLE FAMILY
FROM THE HOSPITAL PAPERS

Scan it once.
Then it’s
handled.

Take a photo of the discharge papers. Adltrack reads the medications, doses and times, and you check each one before anything is saved.

Every medication keeps its photo, its pharmacy and the doctor who prescribed it, so anyone on the team can find them.

WHEN IT’S TIME

A reminder.
Then a tick.

The person giving the dose gets the reminder, taps Done or Skip, and the whole family sees it. If a dose is missed, everyone hears about it.

HOW IT WORKS
01

Scan the papers.

Photograph the discharge papers and confirm each medication.

02

Get reminded.

A reminder at every dose time, even when the app is closed.

03

Never run out.

Mark a medication Finished or Needs refill, and Adltrack reminds you to call the doctor.
