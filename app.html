HOW ADLTRACK WORKS

Bring them home. Keep them on track.

  1. Scan. Take a photo of the discharge papers or the pharmacy labels.
  2. Confirm. Check each medication against the papers and the bottle. Nothing is reminded until you confirm it.
  3. Take. My Tasks shows every dose at its time. Mark it Done or Skip, and the whole care team sees it.
  4. Refill. A week before a medication runs out, Adltrack reminds you to call the pharmacy or the doctor.
Adltrack doesn’t give medical advice. It only shows what the papers and the labels say. For anything else, call the doctor or the pharmacist. In an emergency, call 911.
